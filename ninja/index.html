Questions

Short answers, no sales talk.

Who exactly screens my candidate?
A former Amazon Bar Raiser working under the codename KAGE-01. Codenames protect experts who are currently employed elsewhere. After you pay, you receive their real name, photo and LinkedIn before the call is scheduled.
Why not just use an AI screening tool?
Because candidates use AI too. Automated tools grade the answer, not the person giving it. A live conversation with follow-up questions is currently the only reliable way to tell real experience from a well-written script.
What if the candidate is great and we hire them?
Nothing changes — we charge per screen, not per hire. We are not a recruiting agency and take no placement fee.
How fast is it really?
The call is scheduled within 24 hours of payment; the report arrives within 48 hours of the call. If we cannot schedule within that window, you get a full refund.
What do you do with the candidate's data?
The CV is used only for the screen and is deleted on request. The report goes to you alone. We never publish candidate profiles or sell data.
Who are you?
A small team building infrastructure for verified human reputation. Verified.Ninja is the first commercial service built on it — the underlying project is WorldConstitution.
