Questions

Who exactly screens my candidate?
A senior engineer from a FAANG-scale company, working under a codename — see the profile above for their exact background. Codenames protect experts who are currently employed elsewhere. After you pay, you receive their real name, photo and LinkedIn before the call is scheduled.
What is a Bar Raiser?
At Amazon, a Bar Raiser is a specially trained interviewer attached to hiring loops outside their own team, with the authority to veto a hire that would lower the company's standard. It is the role built specifically to stop teams from hiring "good enough" under deadline pressure. That's the standard your candidate is measured against.
What is a Shadow Session?
Your team runs its own interview as usual and our expert joins the same call as a silent observer — camera off, no participation. Afterwards you get a 15-minute debrief plus two written assessments: one on the candidate, one on how your team interviewed. $299. We ask you to tell the candidate an observer will be present.
Why not just use an AI screening tool?
Because candidates use AI too. Automated tools grade the answer, not the person giving it. A live conversation with follow-up questions is currently the only reliable way to tell real experience from a well-written script.
What if the candidate is great and we hire them?
Nothing changes — we charge per screen, not per hire. We are not a recruiting agency and take no placement fee.
How fast is it really?
The call is scheduled within 24 hours of payment; the report arrives within 48 hours of the call. If we cannot schedule within that window, you get a full refund.
What do you do with the candidate's data?
The CV is used only for the screen and is deleted on request. The report goes to you alone. We never publish candidate profiles or sell data.
Who are you?
A small team building infrastructure for verified human reputation. Verified.Ninja is the first commercial service built on it — the underlying project is WorldConstitution.
